Applicable attribution guidance verified at https://help.streamlinehq.com/en/articles/5354376-streamline-free-license . Attribution is visible in the app footer/sidebar and settings. The public official repository provides selected free vector assets; this application is not an icon redistribution library.

Instrument Serif and Instrument Sans are SIL Open Font License fonts. Their license notices are bundled as `public/assets/instrumentserif-OFL.txt` and `instrumentsans-OFL.txt`. The local WOFF2 Latin subsets correspond to the fonts observed at https://ui.unlumen.com/ . They load from this application, without contacting the reference site or a CDN at runtime. Korean falls back to system fonts.

The reference site's layout was observed for design interpretation; no proprietary component source or branded product content was copied. The abstract typographic study on Atelier's start screen is original HTML/CSS.
